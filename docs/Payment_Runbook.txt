Payment Gateway Timeout

Symptoms:
- Customers cannot complete payment.
- Orders may remain in Pending status.
- Repeated timeout messages appear in application logs.

Possible causes:
- The external payment provider is unavailable.
- Network communication with the provider failed.
- The payment request exceeded its timeout limit.

Recommended actions:
- Check the payment provider's service status.
- Retry the payment request.
- Review pending orders before retrying payment.
- Escalate to the payment provider if failures continue.
